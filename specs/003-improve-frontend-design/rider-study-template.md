## SC-001: Find a Stop and Read the Next Bus

**Task prompt**: "Find the stop named {stop name} and tell me the next bus and its destination." Do not coach or reveal the route through the interface.

1. Start timing when the app is fully open and the task prompt is read.
2. Stop timing when the participant identifies the first upcoming bus and destination, or at 30 seconds.
3. Pass = completed accurately within 30 seconds. Record elapsed time, assistance, errors, and participant comments.
4. Success threshold = at least 9 of 10 participants pass.
